Test log

Every firing, on the record.

We publish every hot-fire and flight, including the ones that didn't go to plan.

  1. Mar 2024HF-001

    First light

    3.2 s burn to characterize the injector. Shut down on schedule.

    Pass
  2. Sep 2024HF-019

    Full duration

    18.4 s at full thrust. Chamber pressure within 2% of the model.

    Pass
  3. Dec 2024HF-031

    Nozzle erosion

    Throat eroded past limits. We moved to silica-phenolic and confirmed the fix on HF-034.

    Anomaly
  4. Feb 2025HF-047

    Pass
